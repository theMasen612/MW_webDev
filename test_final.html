The Gateway – Salt Lake City

A fun place to explore, shop, eat, and make memories!

Welcome to The Gateway!

The Gateway is a fun shopping and entertainment destination in downtown Salt Lake City, Utah. It has restaurants, shops, museums, and places to enjoy with family and friends. There is something for everyone to explore!

Why Visit The Gateway?

The Gateway is a great place to visit because you can do many different activities all in one location.

  • Visit Clark Planetarium and explore space.
  • Shop for candy at Candy Barrel.
  • Explore fun exhibits at Discovery Gateway Children's Museum.
  • Watch a movie at Megaplex Theatres.
  • Enjoy a comedy show at Wiseguys.
  • Explore the different stores and restaurants.

Clark Planetarium

Clark Planetarium is a fun place to learn about space, planets, stars, and the universe. Visitors can explore exhibits and enjoy exciting planetarium experiences.

Candy Barrel

Candy Barrel is a colorful candy shop where visitors can find different kinds of sweets and treats. It is a fun stop for anyone who loves candy!

Uptown Cheapskate

Uptown Cheapskate is a secondhand clothing store where shoppers can look for clothing and accessories. It is a place to explore different styles.

Discovery Gateway Children's Museum

Discovery Gateway is a children's museum with interactive exhibits and activities. It gives kids a chance to learn, create, and explore through hands-on experiences.

Megaplex Theatres

Megaplex Theatres is a movie theater at The Gateway. It is a fun place to watch a movie and enjoy a night out.

Wiseguys Comedy Club

Wiseguys is a comedy club where visitors can enjoy live comedy performances. It is a fun place to go for a night of entertainment.

Want to Learn More?

The Gateway is located in downtown Salt Lake City. Whether you want to shop, eat, learn something new, or have fun with friends, The Gateway has plenty of things to explore!
