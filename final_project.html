Welcome to The Gateway!

The Gateway is a fun entertainment and shopping destination located in downtown Salt Lake City, Utah. It has restaurants, stores, museums, entertainment, and fun places to visit. Whether you want to go shopping, watch a movie, explore a museum, or spend time with friends and family, The Gateway has something for everyone. It is a great place to visit when traveling to Utah!

Places to Visit at The Gateway

  • Clark Planetarium
  • Candy Barrel
  • Uptown Cheapskate
  • Discovery Gateway Children's Museum
  • Megaplex Theatres
  • Wiseguys Comedy Club

Here are six fun places to explore during your visit!

Clark Planetarium

Clark Planetarium is a great place to learn about space, planets, stars, and the universe. Visitors can explore interesting exhibits and watch educational shows. It is a fun experience for anyone who enjoys science and discovering new things.

Candy Barrel

Candy Barrel is a fun place to stop if you have a sweet tooth. You can find different kinds of candy and treats to enjoy. It is a great place to visit with friends or family while exploring The Gateway.

Uptown Cheapskate

Uptown Cheapskate is a fun thrift shopping store where visitors can look for clothing and accessories. It is a great place to explore different styles and find something unique.

Discovery Gateway Children's Museum

Discovery Gateway Children's Museum is an interactive museum where kids can learn while having fun. It has hands-on exhibits that encourage creativity, imagination, and exploring new things. It is a great place for families to visit.

Megaplex Theatres

Megaplex Theatres is a great place to watch movies and spend time with friends or family. You can enjoy a movie, get snacks, and relax after exploring the other attractions at The Gateway.

Wiseguys Comedy Club

Wiseguys Comedy Club is a fun place to enjoy live comedy and entertainment. Visitors can watch comedians perform and enjoy a night of laughter. It is a unique experience for people looking for something different to do.

Want to Learn More?

The Gateway is located in downtown Salt Lake City and is a great place to spend a day exploring. You can visit different attractions, shop, eat, and enjoy entertainment all in one area. There is always something new to discover!
