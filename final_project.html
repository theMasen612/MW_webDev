Welcome to The Gateway!

The Gateway is a popular shopping and entertainment destination located in downtown Salt Lake City, Utah. It has restaurants, stores, museums, and fun activities for people of all ages. Whether you want to shop, watch a movie, or explore something new, The Gateway has something for everyone.

Why Visit The Gateway?

  • Enjoy shopping at different stores.
  • Watch a movie at Megaplex Luxury Theatres.
  • Visit museums and learn something new.
  • Try different restaurants and places to eat.
  • Have fun with friends and family.

Explore Even More!

The Gateway has many different places to explore. You can spend the day shopping, eating, visiting attractions, or enjoying entertainment. It is a great place to make a whole day out of your visit to Salt Lake City!

Megaplex Luxury Theatres

Megaplex at The Gateway is a great place to watch a movie. It is a fun activity to do with friends or family, especially when you want to relax and enjoy a new movie.

Wiseguys Comedy Club

Wiseguys Comedy Club is a fun place to enjoy live comedy. You can watch comedians perform and spend an entertaining evening laughing with friends or family.

Discovery Gateway Children's Museum

Discovery Gateway Children's Museum is a place where kids can learn through hands-on activities and interactive exhibits. It is a fun place for families to explore, create, and discover new things together.

Clark Planetarium

Clark Planetarium is a great place to learn about space, planets, and the universe. Visitors can explore exhibits and discover interesting facts about astronomy and science.
